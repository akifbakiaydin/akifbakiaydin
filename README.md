### 👋 About me

I design and bring up electronic hardware — from schematic and PCB layout to firmware, testing and field deployment in industrial environments.
Alongside hardware, I handle IT infrastructure: workstations, networks and servers that keep production running.

- 🛠️ **Focus:** reliable, manufacturable boards for industrial use
- ⚡ **Approach:** from first schematic to working hardware in the field

---

### 🧰 What I do

| Area | Details |
|---|---|
| **PCB Design** | Schematic capture, multi-layer layout, DFM/DFA, BOM & Gerber preparation |

| **Electronics** | Power supplies, sensor interfaces, prototyping, soldering & rework, debugging |
| **Industrial Hardware** | Control boards, field wiring, PLC/sensor integration, commissioning |
